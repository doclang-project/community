# Intellectual Property Policy

> **Status:** draft — pending review and approval by the founding members and project legal counsel before use. This file establishes the *intent* of the policy; final, binding terms will be incorporated by reference from the hosting foundation's standard IP framework once selected.

This policy describes the licensing and patent terms that apply to the DocLang specification, its reference implementations, and contributions made to the project. It is structured to support submission of the specification to a formal standards body (e.g. ISO).

## 1. Copyright licensing

### 1.1 Specification text

The DocLang specification and all accompanying documentation are licensed under the [Apache License, Version 2.0](./LICENSE), with the understanding that implementations conforming to the specification may be developed by anyone, royalty-free.

### 1.2 Reference implementations and tooling

All source code in the DocLang project (reference implementations, validators, tooling, schemas) is licensed under the [Apache License, Version 2.0](./LICENSE).

## 2. Contributions

### 2.1 Developer Certificate of Origin

Every contribution must include a DCO sign-off in the commit message (`Signed-off-by:`), as described in [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md). The DCO is the [Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1](https://developercertificate.org/), the same one used by the Linux kernel.

### 2.2 Inbound contribution license

By submitting a contribution to the project, the contributor licenses their contribution to the project — and downstream users — under the same Apache 2.0 terms as the surrounding work. No copyright assignment is required.

## 3. Patent commitments

For DocLang to be safely adoptable as an open standard, contributors and member companies grant the following commitments. These mirror the Apache 2.0 patent grant and the W3C Royalty-Free patent licensing model commonly used for standards work.

### 3.1 Apache 2.0 patent grant

Every contribution carries the patent grant of [Apache 2.0 Section 3](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0#patent): each contributor grants a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, no-charge, royalty-free, irrevocable patent license to make, have made, use, offer to sell, sell, import, and otherwise transfer their contribution, where such license applies only to those patent claims licensable by the contributor that are necessarily infringed by their contribution alone or by combination of their contribution with the work to which it was submitted.

### 3.2 Founding-member RF commitment

Each founding member commits, with respect to its Essential Claims, to grant a royalty-free, non-discriminatory license to any party implementing the DocLang specification, under terms substantially similar to the W3C Royalty-Free Patent Licensing Mode.

"Essential Claims" means claims of patents owned or controlled by the member that are necessarily infringed by an implementation that conforms to the specification, where there is no commercially reasonable non-infringing alternative.

### 3.3 Defensive termination

The patent license granted to any party terminates as to that party if they initiate patent litigation against the project, against any contributor, or against any implementer of the specification, alleging that the specification or a conforming implementation infringes a patent.

## 4. Trademarks

"DocLang" is intended to be a project trademark. Use of the name and any associated logos is governed by a trademark policy maintained by the hosting foundation. Until the hosting foundation is confirmed, use of the name should be limited to:

- Accurate, non-misleading references to the specification (e.g. "supports DocLang", "implements DocLang").
- Project communications by member companies acting in their member capacity.

## 5. Disclosure obligations

Members are expected to disclose, in good faith, patents they believe to be Essential to the specification, in line with the disclosure requirements of typical standards-setting bodies (e.g. ISO, W3C). Disclosures should be sent to the TSC via the contact in [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md).

## 6. Amendments

This policy may be amended by a two-thirds vote of the TSC, subject to review by project legal counsel and the hosting foundation. Amendments do not apply retroactively to contributions already made under prior terms.
